ReelForge Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

ReelForge is a private tool used only by its owner to upload and manage videos on the owner's own YouTube channel.

What we access

When you sign in with Google, ReelForge requests permission to manage YouTube videos on your account. It uses this only to upload and update your own videos.

What we store

ReelForge stores only the sign-in tokens needed to keep working, inside the owner's Google Apps Script project. We do not collect any other personal data.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. ReelForge's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can remove ReelForge's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
